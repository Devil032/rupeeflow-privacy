# rupeeflow-privacy# RupeeFlow — Offline AI Expense & Budget Manager

**RupeeFlow** is a fast, 100% offline personal expense manager, budget tracker, and udhaar ledger built natively for Android using Kotlin & Jetpack Compose.

## Key Features
- **Offline AI Smart Quick-Entry:** Type natural entries like `"Chai 20 UPI"` or `"Petrol 300 cash"` to auto-detect amount, category, and payment mode.
- **1-Tap Quick Chips:** Instantly log frequent daily expenses with one tap.
- **Monthly Budget Alerts:** Visual progress bar with an 80% overspending warning.
- **Udhaar / Dues Tracker:** Track money lent to or borrowed from friends and settle in one tap.
- **Biometric App Lock:** Secure your ledger using your phone's Fingerprint or Device PIN.
- **Bilingual Support:** Switch the interface and AI insights between English and Hindi anytime.
- **1-Click CSV Export:** Backup or share your transaction history as an Excel-ready `.csv` file.
- **100% Offline & Private:** All financial records stay locally on your device.
