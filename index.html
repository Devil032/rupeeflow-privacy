Privacy Policy for RupeeFlow

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

RupeeFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is an offline-first personal expense manager, budget tracker, and udhaar ledger. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how your information is handled.

1. Local Data Storage

All financial transactions, monthly budgets, notes, and udhaar records you enter in RupeeFlow are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal financial records on any external server.

2. App Security & Biometric Authentication

If you enable the optional Fingerprint or Device PIN App Lock, authentication is handled securely by your Android operating system. RupeeFlow never accesses or stores your biometric data or device PIN.

3. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

RupeeFlow uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use standard device identifiers, advertising IDs, and diagnostic data to serve relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Backup & Export

You can export your transaction history as a CSV file directly to your device storage or share it using apps of your choice. You retain full control over your exported files.

5. Children's Privacy

RupeeFlow is intended for general audiences aged 16 and above and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
